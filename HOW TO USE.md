# REDEFINE Life Game: Agent Guide

A game you play **with** people, not a presentation you give **at** them.

**App link:** https://wkahjian93.github.io/redefine-life-game/

---

## Before your first game (one time, 2 minutes)

1. Open the app link on your phone, then tap the browser menu and choose **Add to Home screen**. It now works without internet.
2. Tap **⚙️ Settings** and enter **your own name, WhatsApp number and agency name**. Save.
   Quick Mode WhatsApp messages come to *you*, and your name goes on every card and summary.
3. Leave the **illustrative assumptions** alone unless you can explain why you changed them.
4. Tap **📚 Sources** once so you know where every number comes from. Prospects will ask.

Top bar, on every screen: **⌂** home · **中文 / EN** switch language at any time · **🔊 / 🔇** all sound on/off. Background music starts on the first tap; turn just the music off in **⚙️ Settings → Background music** (handy in a quiet café).

## ⚡ Quick Mode: strangers (60–90 seconds)

**When:** campus booths, events, cafés, roadshows, cold approaches. Anywhere you need a fun reason for someone to stop.

**Opening line (pick one):**
- "Quick game, 60 seconds: heart bypass or a new Myvi, which costs more? Swipe to guess 😄"
- "60 秒小游戏：心脏搭桥和一辆全新 Myvi，哪个比较贵？滑一下来猜 😄"

**How it runs (Higher or Lower):**
1. Tap **Quick Game** and hand them the phone (it works one-handed).
2. 3 cards. Each card puts a real cost next to something everyday (a Myvi, an iPhone, a typical family's monthly income). They **swipe up for Higher, down for Lower**, or tap the big buttons.
3. Each card has a **10-second timer**. A right answer scores 100 points, plus up to 50 for speed and a bonus for a 🔥 streak. Not every answer is "Higher": every game has at least one "Lower".
4. After each card the real figure is revealed with its source, then one quick "what would you do?" tap.
5. They get a **money personality card with their score**. Ask them to **Save image** or **Share**: the share message says "Can you beat me?" with the game link.
6. Hand the iPad back to the form under the card: **they type their first name and WhatsApp number and tick the consent box** themselves. Tap **WhatsApp my result**: *your* WhatsApp opens a chat to *their* number with their personality, score and an invitation to a free 5-minute check. **You press send.**
   A chooser appears: **WhatsApp**, **WhatsApp Business**, or **Copy message** (paste into any app). On iPhone/iPad, if WhatsApp Business does not open, the message is copied for you to paste into a new chat there.

**Tip:** for older or slower-paced prospects, turn the timer off in **⚙️ Settings → Quick game timer**.

**Your follow-up:** when they reply YES, answer the same day, mention their personality type, and offer the 5-minute check, or a Full Mode game over coffee. Send the card image too (Save image, then attach it in the chat).

**Rules:**
- **Let the prospect type their own number and tick the box themselves.** That tick is their consent to be contacted; never tick it for them.
- The app does not save the number. It is cleared from the screen the moment WhatsApp opens; the chat lives only in your WhatsApp.
- If they don't want to share their number, that's fine. They can still AirDrop or share the card to their own phone.

## 🧬 Full Mode: customers and friends (10–15 minutes, together)

**When:** your fresh excuse to meet existing clients and friends: "I've got a new life game. Want to try? It takes 15 minutes and you'll find out which of your dreams survive."

Sit **side by side**. Let **them** tap. You are the game host, not the presenter.

| Step | What happens | Your job |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Money machine | "You are your family's money machine" | Read the line out loud with energy |
| 2. Dreams | They pick 3 dreams in order, then put a rough RM price on each (their own estimate) | Ask "why this one?" Listen. This is the real conversation |
| 3. Setup | Age, take-home income, commitments, spending, savings, EPF, loans, children, parents, existing cover | Rough numbers are fine. Not sure about cover? Put 0 and offer to check their policies later |
| 4. Card mode | **What if** (you pick the card) or **Real odds** (random, real odds only) | Use **What if** to explore one scenario on purpose. Say clearly: "This is a what-if, not a prediction." |
| 5. 3 life stages | Each stage they split income across 🛍️ lifestyle / 🐷 savings / 📈 investment / 🛡️ protection, then a life card is drawn | Let them choose. If they set protection to 0%, the app shows their policies lapsing. Let them discover that |
| 6. Scorecard | Which dreams survived, money lost, insurance paid, what the family is left with at 60 | Pause. Ask "How do you feel about this?" |
| 7. Before vs after | Same life replayed with the gaps covered | **Type the monthly cost from the latest approved quotation** in the dashed box. Without it, After looks better than reality and the app warns about it |
| 8. Needs summary | One page: dreams, scorecard, before/after, gaps in RM | Tap any gap row (**ƒx**) to show the formula. Save as image or PDF and send it to them |

**Which cards to explore (What if):**
- 📈 Promotion · 👶 New baby · 🏥 Hospital stay · 💔 Critical illness · ⛔ Income stops 2 years · ☀️ Smooth years
- 🕯️ **Money machine stops for good** (death / total disability): use it gently, only with people who are ready for that conversation.

**After the game:**
- **🧹 New game** wipes this player's answers from your phone. Always tap it before the next person.
- The game names **no products**. Product discussion happens after, by you, with approved documents.

## Honesty rules (why people trust this game)

- Every real figure has a source (**📚 Sources**). Figures are ranges or medians, never worst cases dressed up as normal.
- If they are already covered, the app says **"✅ You're covered here"**. Say it out loud too. That builds more trust than any pitch.
- **Real odds** mode often gives a calm life. That's the honest result; don't switch modes just to scare.
- Never promise returns, guaranteed acceptance or specific premiums. The returns in the game are illustrative and editable.
- Player data stays on your phone only, and **New game** clears it. Don't screenshot other people's details to your group chats.

## FAQ

**"Is this financial advice?"** No. It's education. The gaps are rule-based estimates. A proper needs analysis with official documents comes next.
**"Where do these numbers come from?"** Tap 📚 Sources: DOSM, EPF, the National Cancer Registry, LIAM/MTA/PIAM, RinggitPlus and others, each with its date.
**"Why is my retirement covered?"** The projection uses illustrative returns and is **not adjusted for inflation**, so treat a ✅ there as a good sign, not a guarantee.
**No internet?** Once opened (or added to the Home screen) it works offline. WhatsApp sending needs signal.

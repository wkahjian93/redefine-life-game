## Design rules for every game

- **3–5 minutes**, on the agent's iPad, played side by side. The customer taps; the agent tells the story.
- **One "feeling moment" per game**: the screen pauses on the line that should land, and the agent card shows the line to say.
- **Agent script button** (🎤) on every screen: your lesson's own words, in English and 中文.
- **Customer's own numbers**: income, savings, medical card limit, sum assured. Nothing is invented, and nothing is stored after the game.
- **Honest**: costs come from approved figures only (F1–F13); if the customer is already covered, the game says so. No product names, no premiums.
- Ends with **one closing question** from your lesson and a button straight into the Full Life Game or a needs summary.

## Proposed games (7)

### Medical (2 games)

**M1. "Insure it or not?" (保险分类游戏)**: Chapter 1 + 2 + 灭火器/栏杆
- 8 cards appear one by one (flu, phone screen, car scratch, heart bypass RM70k–120k, cancer up to RM395k, hospital stay RM10,700, stroke stay RM15,671, house fire). The customer swipes each into **"I can pay this myself"** or **"I can't afford to lose this"**.
- Then the cards jump onto a 2×2 board: **how likely** × **how serious**. Flu sits in "likely, small"; heart bypass sits in "unlikely, huge".
- **Feeling moment:** "We don't insure what is likely. We insure what we can't afford to lose." (Fire extinguisher line follows.)
- Fun: swipe speed, card flip sounds, a score for "sorted like a planner".

**M2. "The Organ Robber" (强盗抢器官)**: the strongest story in the lesson
- A cartoon robber offers to buy the customer's liver, eyes, heart. The customer sets **their own selling price** on a slider ("Would you sell for RM1m? 2m?"). Most refuse at any price; the app counts their refusals.
- The robber "steals" anyway and sells them back **at the price they named** → a total bill appears.
- Then: "In real life the robber is illness and accidents." The customer enters their **medical card limit**, and the game shows how much of the buy-back bill they could pay today. If their limit covers it, it says so.
- **Feeling moment:** "Do you already have this money ready?"
- Bonus round (optional, 30 s): **红白键**: red button = sure RM1m, white = 50% RM10m or lose RM1m. Almost everyone presses red → "That's why we buy a medical card."

### Income protection (2 games)

**I1. "Sell your earning power" (Mr Death/TPD/CI auction)**
- An auction screen: "Mr. TPD wants to buy your ability to work. Will you sell for RM100k? RM500k? RM1m?" The customer keeps pressing **NO** as the bids rise (with gavel sounds), then names **their** price.
- The app shows the real value of what they would be selling: monthly income × 12 × years to retirement (their numbers).
- "Now open your policy: what is your sum assured?" They enter it, and a big price tag drops: **"SALE: 92% OFF"** (sum assured ÷ value). If they have no cover: **"FREE GIVEAWAY"**. If the cover is close to the value, it says "fair price ✅".
- **Feeling moment:** "Don't sell something priceless at a discount."

**I2. "The Money Tree" (大树)**
- The customer grows a tree: each tap = a month of salary rain; fruits appear for their goals (house, car, children's education, parents). In ~40 seconds the tree is full and beautiful.
- A life card stops the rain (critical illness / TPD; the agent picks it, like Full Mode "What if"). The tree starts dropping fruits one by one to pay the family's monthly spending (their number), with a counter of months survived.
- Replay with **income protection as a water tank**: lump sum = their CI/TPD cover; the tree keeps its fruit for N more months.
- **Feeling moment:** "We protected the fruit with a medical card, but forgot to protect the root: the salary." Optional warranty quiz first: "How many years of warranty does your phone have? Your car? Your salary?"

### Savings (2 games)

**S1. "Temptation Jar" (存一下花一下 / spending vs savings account)**
- Two jars on screen: **ATM jar** (open lid) and **Savings jar** (locked). Each month coins drop in automatically.
- Temptation pop-ups fly in fast (sale!, new phone, trip, "treat yourself"); the customer must tap **Resist** or **Spend**. Money in the ATM jar can be grabbed by temptations; the locked jar can't.
- After "10 years" (≈60 seconds), compare jars, using only the amounts they chose. Then Kah Jian's mum line: "If it would have been spent anyway, saving it = 100% gained."
- **Feeling moment:** "收入 − 储蓄 = 开销. Save first, spend what's left."

**S2. "The Monk's Well" (和尚挖井)** + **"What's the key?" quiz**
- Two monks: each day the customer chooses for Monk B "fetch water" or "fetch water + dig 5 minutes". A 3-year fast-forward; then a drought day: Monk A walks down the hill while Monk B relaxes by his own well.
- Quiz: "RM1,000 a month, 6%, 30 years: which is the key?" The answer reveals "30 years uninterrupted". The compounding chart uses **illustrative returns only**, labelled as such.
- **Feeling moment:** "以后的成就取决于你今天的眼光."
